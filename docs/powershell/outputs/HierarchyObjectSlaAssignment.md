### HierarchyObjectSlaAssignment
Containing calculated information about SLA assignment on a particular
object.

- assignmentType: SlaAssignmentType
  - Assignment type tells the type of SLA assignment.
- effectiveSla: EffectiveSlaAssignment
  - The effective SLA on the object.
- sla: SlaAssignmentDetail
  - The SLA assigned to this object.
