### IdentityInfo
Information about an identity, including its name, type, status, and IdP details.

- identityName: System.String
  - Name of the identity.
- identityType: PrincipalRiskySummaryPrincipalType
  - Type of the identity.
- privilegeType: PrivilegeType
  - Privilege type of the identity.
- entityId: System.String
  - Entity ID.
- entityName: System.String
  - Entity name, such as a domain name or cloud account name.
- idpType: IdpType
  - IdP type.
- uniqueIdentifier: System.String
  - The unique identifier of the identity.
- status: IdentityStatus
  - Status of the identity.
