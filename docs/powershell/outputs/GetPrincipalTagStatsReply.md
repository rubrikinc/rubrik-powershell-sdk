### GetPrincipalTagStatsReply
GetPrincipalTagStatsReply contains the aggregated statistics for principal
tags.

- privileged: PrincipalTagStats
  - Aggregated statistics for privileged tag.
- atrisk: PrincipalTagStats
  - Aggregated statistics for at-risk tag.
- sensitive: PrincipalTagStats
  - Aggregated statistics for sensitive tag.
