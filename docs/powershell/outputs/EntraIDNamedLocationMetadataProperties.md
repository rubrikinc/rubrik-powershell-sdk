### EntraIDNamedLocationMetadataProperties
EntraIDNamedLocationMetadataProperties holds additional properties for
EntraID named locations.

- locationType: EntraIDNamedLocationType
  - The type of named location (IP-based or country-based).
- createdDateTime: System.String
  - The date and time when the named location was created.
- modifiedDateTime: System.String
  - The date and time when the named location was last modified.
- properties: PropertiesOneof
