### IrisdbVolume
A storage volume backing the IRIS DB instance.

- volumeId: System.String
  - The volume identifier on the storage array.
- name: System.String
  - The volume name reported by the storage array.
- mountPath: System.String
  - The mount path on the IRIS host.
- sizeInBytes: System.Int64
  - The provisioned volume size in bytes.
