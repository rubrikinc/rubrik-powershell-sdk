### ViolationInfo
ViolationInfo represents the violation information.

- violationSeverity: ViolationSeverity
  - Violation severity.
- totalCount: System.Int32
  - Total violation count.
- criticalCount: System.Int32
  - Critical violation count.
- highCount: System.Int32
  - High violation count.
- mediumCount: System.Int32
  - Medium violation count.
- lowCount: System.Int32
  - Low violation count.
