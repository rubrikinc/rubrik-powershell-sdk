### AdContactMetadata
On-prem AD contact-specific metadata.

- email: System.String
  - Specifies the email address of the contact.
- telephone: System.String
  - Specifies the telephone number of the contact.
- office: System.String
  - Specifies the office location of the contact.
- organisation: System.String
  - Specifies the organisation of the contact.
- company: System.String
  - Specifies the company of the contact.
