### EntraIDRoleProperties
EntraIDRoleProperties contains properties specific to EntraID roles.

- roleDescription: System.String
  - Represents the role description.
- type: EntraIDRoleType
  - Represents the role type.
- isPrivileged: System.Boolean
  - Represents whether the role is privileged or not.
- hasRiskyPermissions: System.Boolean
  - Represents whether the role has risky permissions.
