### MssqlRecoveryPlansInfo
Information about a Recovery Plan that includes a SQL Server database.

- id: System.String
  - The unique identifier of the Recovery Plan.
- name: System.String
  - The name of the Recovery Plan.
- recoveryType: System.String
  - The type of the Recovery Plan.
- recoveryPlanType: RecoveryPlanType
  - The type of the Recovery Plan.
