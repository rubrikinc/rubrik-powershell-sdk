### PolicyInsight
PolicyInsight categorizes the policy insight a GPO change surfaces (e.g. a
Kerberos-Policy change). It is the single classification stamped at
ingestion and used as the filter value on the activity-log, report, and
alert pages, and as the label on the GPO change detail view.

- POLICY_INSIGHT_UNSPECIFIED - Default, unset policy insight.
- POLICY_INSIGHT_KERBEROS_POLICY - The GPO change touched an Account Policies/Kerberos Policy setting.
