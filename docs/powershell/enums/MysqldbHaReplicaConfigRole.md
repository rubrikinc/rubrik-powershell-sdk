### MysqldbHaReplicaConfigRole
User-intended role hint for a replica in an HA MySQL cluster. PRIMARY identifies the writable primary; REPLICA identifies a read replica. Used only as the initial seeded role - discovery confirms or corrects the actual role at runtime.

- MYSQLDB_HA_REPLICA_CONFIG_ROLE_PRIMARY
- MYSQLDB_HA_REPLICA_CONFIG_ROLE_REPLICA
