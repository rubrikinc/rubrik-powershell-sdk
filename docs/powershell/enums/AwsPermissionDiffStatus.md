### AwsPermissionDiffStatus
AwsPermissionDiffStatus says whether a diff could be computed.

- AWS_PERMISSION_DIFF_STATUS_UNSPECIFIED - Default unspecified value.
- AWS_PERMISSION_DIFF_STATUS_AVAILABLE - The diff was computed. Empty lists mean nothing changes.
- AWS_PERMISSION_DIFF_STATUS_NOT_AVAILABLE - The diff could not be computed; the lists are empty.
