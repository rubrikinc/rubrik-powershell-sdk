### NotificationResourceSubtype
The resource subtype associated with notifications and resources.

- RESOURCE_SUBTYPE_UNSPECIFIED - Unspecified.
- CROSS_ACCOUNT_CLUSTER - Cross-account cluster.
- CROSS_ACCOUNT_PAIR - Cross-account pair.
- MSSQL_DISCOVERY - MSSQL resource discovery.
- SENTRY_AI_ACCOUNT_INSIGHT - A Sentry AI account-level insight.
- SENTRY_AI_CLUSTER_INSIGHT - A Sentry AI cluster-level insight.
- MVB_RECOVERY_ANALYSIS - MVB recovery analysis.
- RESTORE_IMPACT_ANALYSIS - Restore impact analysis.
- MVC_RECOVERY_ANALYSIS - Minimum viable company recovery analysis.
- EXOCOMPUTE_CLUSTER_READY - Exocompute cluster ready for data samples.
