### RecoveryTag
Tag annotations on recovery plans and recoveries. Tags are derived from the
recovery spec, not supplied by callers.

- SQL_HOST_PROVISIONED - The recovery plan or recovery provisions a SQL host.
- HYPERV - The recovery plan or recovery recovers Hyper-V virtual machines to a
target other than Azure Local, such as a standalone Hyper-V host.
- AZURE_LOCAL - The recovery plan or recovery recovers Hyper-V virtual machines only to
Azure Local.
