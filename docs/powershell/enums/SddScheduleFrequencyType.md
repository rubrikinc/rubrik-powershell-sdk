### SddScheduleFrequencyType
Unit of time a recurrence is counted in.

- FREQUENCY_TYPE_UNSPECIFIED - Unspecified frequency type.
- HOURLY - Recurs on an hourly cycle.
- DAILY - Recurs on a daily cycle.
- WEEKLY - Recurs on a weekly cycle.
- MONTHLY - Recurs on a monthly cycle.
- QUARTERLY - Recurs on a quarterly cycle.
- YEARLY - Recurs on a yearly cycle.
