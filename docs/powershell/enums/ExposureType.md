### ExposureType
ExposureType enumerates the possible categories of exposure for data
sensitivity assessments.

- EXPOSURE_TYPE_UNSPECIFIED - Unspecified default exposure type.
- EXPOSURE_TYPE_PUBLIC - Sensitive data exposure accessible publicly without restrictions.
- EXPOSURE_TYPE_ORG_WIDE - Sensitive data exposure accessible to everyone within the organization.
- EXPOSURE_TYPE_EXTERNAL - Sensitive data exposure accessible to external identities
(outside the organization).
