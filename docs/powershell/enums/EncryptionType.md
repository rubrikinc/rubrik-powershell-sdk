### EncryptionType
Encryption type for the data location.

- ENCRYPTION_TYPE_RUBRIK - Rubrik-managed encryption.
- ENCRYPTION_TYPE_BYOK - Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) encryption.
- ENCRYPTION_TYPE_NON_UEM_BYOK - BYOK encryption without UEM management. The customer provided their own
keys outside the UEM flow; rekey and rotation are not available.
