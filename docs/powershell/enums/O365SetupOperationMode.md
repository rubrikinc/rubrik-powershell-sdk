### O365SetupOperationMode
Operation mode for the M365 SaaS setup flow.

- O365_SETUP_OPERATION_MODE_UNSPECIFIED - Unspecified operation mode.
- O365_SETUP_OPERATION_MODE_ONBOARDING_MODE - Standard onboarding mode.
- O365_SETUP_OPERATION_MODE_PRIORITIZED_ONBOARDING - Prioritized onboarding mode, in which a selected subset of objects is
onboarded ahead of the rest.
