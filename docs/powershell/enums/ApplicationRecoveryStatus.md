### ApplicationRecoveryStatus
Operational status of an application recovery.

- RECOVERY_STATUS_UNSPECIFIED - Status is not yet known.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_QUEUED - Recovery is queued for execution.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_ONGOING - Recovery is in progress.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_PAUSED - Recovery is paused.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_CLEANUP_STARTED - Cleanup has started for this recovery.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_CLEANUP_SUCCEEDED - Cleanup has succeeded for this recovery.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_CLEANUP_FAILED - Cleanup has failed for this recovery.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_LOCKED - Recovery is locked.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_COMPLETED - Recovery has completed.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_DONE - Recovery is done.
- RECOVERY_STATUS_INIT - Recovery has been initialized.
