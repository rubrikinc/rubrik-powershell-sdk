### DtaProduct
A Data Threat Analytics product whose per-workload alert setting is being
read or written.

- DTA_PRODUCT_UNSPECIFIED - No product was specified. The server applies the Anomaly Detection
setting.
- DTA_PRODUCT_ANOMALY_DETECTION - Anomaly Detection (Data Threat Analytics).
- DTA_PRODUCT_THREAT_MONITORING - Threat Monitoring.
