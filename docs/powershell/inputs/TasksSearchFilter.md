### TasksSearchFilter
Parameters for tasks search.

- searchKeywordFilter: TasksSearchKeywordFilter
  - Filters on keywords.
- searchObjectFilter: TasksSearchObjectFilter
  - Filters on object type.
- dueDate: DateTime
  - Filters on due date.
- lambdaFilters: LambdaPathFilters
  - Used for Lambda search/browse and diff/full FMD paths for Tasks.
- skipRifItems: System.Boolean
  - Specifies whether or not to skip items in Recoverable Items folder.
